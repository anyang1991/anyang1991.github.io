深远海观测对长续航水面平台需求增长，无人帆船凭借风能驱动与良好水声特性成为重要补充；但相对控制研究，设计与优化讨论不足，限制了航速、观测效率与区域通过性。依托“新型水翼无人帆船”课题，本论文从性能视角系统分析设计现状，指出航速是主要短板且受抗倾覆能力制约，并据此提出两条对策：用 CFD 与仿真驱动设计在尖锐对立中求最优权衡；用环境自适应增稳缓和对立以间接提速。

具体贡献包括：（1）基于水动力仿真与增强遗传/邻域信息优化的通用航速评价（VPP）方法，支持缺乏先验时的横向比较；（2）融合贝叶斯优化与知识迁移的仿真驱动总体设计，缓解大设计空间下的采样维数灾难；（3）以被动水翼为增稳机构，结合仿真评估不同展弦比的增稳、增阻与增速效能。相关结果为高性能无人帆船研制提供理论基础与工程储备。

Growing demand for persistent ocean observation elevates wind-powered autonomous sailboats as complements to existing systems, yet design and optimisation lag behind control research, limiting speed, survey efficiency, and area passability. Under the “New Hydrofoil Autonomous Sailboat” project, this thesis reviews designs from a performance perspective, identifies speed as the key bottleneck constrained by capsize resistance, and advances two strategies: CFD- and simulation-driven design to balance the sharp trade-off, and environmentally adaptive stabilisation to ease it and raise speed indirectly.

Contributions include (1) a generalised speed-evaluation (VPP) pipeline combining hydrodynamic simulation with an enhanced genetic solver and neighbourhood-information optimisation; (2) a simulation-driven configuration framework using Bayesian optimisation and knowledge transfer to tame the sampling curse in large design spaces; and (3) passive hydrofoil stabilisers with CFD-based assessment of stability, drag, and speed effects across aspect ratios. Results furnish theory and engineering groundwork for higher-performance autonomous sailboats.
