现代风力航行沿两条主线演化：追求规则约束下极限性能的竞技帆船，以及面向长航时海洋观测的自主机器人帆船。二者物理基础相同，但平台形态、运行目标与发表社群高度分离，关键成果常停留在各自圈层甚至因竞赛保密而难以共享，系统化交叉比较长期缺失。

本文以作者在机器人与竞技两侧的跨域经验为支撑，做专家导向的综合与对照。历史脉络上，竞技侧从评级体系、复合材料、VPP/CFD 工作流到水翼革命；机器人侧则经历概念探索、竞赛驱动的工程化，再到 Saildrone Surveyor 等平台的规模化与任务化。技术上按四级递进展开：部件性能评价支撑整船建模，建模支撑运动控制，控制支撑反应式导航与气象航路规划。每一级用统一五指标（迁移潜力、成熟度领先方、主要受益方、当前迁移程度、主要障碍）评估方法转移。结论揭示关键协同与结构性分歧，并为风助船舶等相邻领域提供可借鉴的对照框架。

Modern wind-driven navigation has bifurcated into competitive racing—maximizing performance under class and event rules—and autonomous robotic sailing for persistent ocean observation. Shared physics notwithstanding, platforms, objectives, and research communities remain largely separate, limiting cross-pollination and leaving few systematic comparative analyses.

Drawing on expertise across both domains, this perspective-driven review traces historical divergence—from rating systems, composites, VPP/CFD workflows, and foiling on the racing side, to the emergent–developmental–application trajectory of sailing robots culminating in large operational platforms such as the Saildrone Surveyor. Technically, it proceeds hierarchically through component performance evaluation, overall vessel modeling, motion control, and high-level reactive navigation and weather-aware routing. A consistent five-metric framework gauges transferability at each level. The synthesis surfaces critical synergies, fundamental divergences, and shared challenges, offering a roadmap for mutual advancement—and for adjacent communities such as wind-assisted ship propulsion.
