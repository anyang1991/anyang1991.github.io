This research provides a comprehensive review of the contrasts and synergies between elite sailing racing and autonomous robotic sailing. It analyzes how racing-grade innovations—such as hydrofoils, advanced materials, and high-fidelity sensors—can be adapted to enhance the efficiency and reliability of maritime robots.

The study highlights how robotic sailing addresses long-term endurance and autonomous decision-making in unpredictable environments, while racing focuses on peak speed and human-machine interaction. By synthesizing these divergent paths, the work outlines a technological roadmap for the next generation of unmanned sailing vessels, fostering collaboration between sports science and ocean engineering.
