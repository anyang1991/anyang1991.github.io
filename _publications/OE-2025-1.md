环境信息对海上作业安全至关重要，波浪是作用于浮体结构的主导外力。波浪浮标类比（WBA）以船舶运动反演现场波浪能谱，成本低、部署灵活，是卫星、浮标与船载雷达之外的重要补充。然而，真实海浪具有显著时变特征；传统做法常在约 30 分钟固定窗内将其近似为平稳过程，忽略时间演化会在响应谱中引入偏差，并在病态反演中被显著放大。

本文提出半平稳演化海况概念，用分段稳态与渐变过渡刻画连续变化的波浪参数，并设计基于小波的自适应分段算法：在时频权衡下分析船舶运动响应谱，识别环境转折点，再对相对平稳的区间分别估计方向波浪谱。动力定位场景下的数值仿真表明，相较固定窗方法，该方案能更稳健地跟踪海况演化。当前研究采用理想 RAO；后续可结合受限等距性质等准则，进一步应对传递函数不确定性。

Accurate environmental awareness is critical for safe marine operations, with waves as the dominant external forcing on floating structures. The wave buoy analogy (WBA) estimates the on-site wave-energy distribution from readily available ship motions, offering a flexible, near-zero-cost complement to satellites, wave-rider buoys, and ship-borne radar. In practice, however, ocean waves are time-involved; approximating them as stationary over fixed windows (commonly ~30 minutes) injects spectral bias that can be strongly amplified by the ill-posed inverse problem underlying WBA.

This study defines semi-stationary evolutionary sea states—stage-wise stationary regimes linked by gradual transitions—and introduces a wavelet-based adaptive segmentation algorithm that balances time and frequency resolution, detects transitions in response spectra, and estimates directional wave spectra on more coherent segments. Simulations in a dynamic-positioning setting show improved tracking of evolving seas relative to fixed-window baselines. Ideal RAOs are assumed here; addressing RAO uncertainty (e.g., via restricted-isometry-type criteria) is identified as future work.
