This research addresses the inaccuracy of traditional wave buoy analogy (WBA) models caused by rigid stationary assumptions in evolving environments. We propose a novel framework using Continuous Wavelet Transformation (CWT) and the PELT algorithm to adaptively segment ship motion data. Unlike fixed-window methods, this approach optimizes the time-frequency trade-off to capture the continuous evolution of wave properties. Validated on a 55m cargo ship across 40 simulation scenarios, the framework accurately identifies temporal transitions in heave, roll, and pitch motions. The results demonstrate significant gains in estimation robustness, reducing Mean Square Error (MSE) by 31% to 56% compared to traditional methods.
