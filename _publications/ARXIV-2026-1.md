This research addresses the Coverage Path Planning (CPP) challenges of autonomous sailboats operating in unpredictable wind and current fields. We introduce a dual-optimization framework that uses topological morphological constraints to ensure spatial compactness and a look-ahead MCTS planner to exploit weather forecasts. Unlike static sweeping patterns that stall in adverse flows, our approach anticipates environmental transitions to maintain high-speed routing. Validated across randomized ocean scenarios, the framework achieves 96.4% coverage while improving efficiency by 30% over baseline strategies. This work establishes a robust foundation for adaptive, long-duration ocean observation.
