无人帆船续航出色，适合长时海洋观测，但航速相对风向显著各向异性，且风流场在空间非均匀、时间上持续演化，导致部分区域在特定时段仅能从有限方向进入。现有覆盖路径规划（CPP）多依赖静态分区与往复扫掠（boustrophedon），难以处理性能各向异性与局部方向不可达；点到点路径规划亦未直接求解覆盖问题。

本文给出面向该设定的时空 CPP 框架。空间侧用拓扑形态约束调控已覆盖区域形状，抑制碎片化与过度折返；时间侧利用气象预报做前瞻决策，在预报不确定性随视距累积的假设下权衡短期效率与长期可达性。环境离散为网格，动作集为 16 向，有效速度由 VPP 与流场叠加得到。随机非均匀时变场景（含部分方向不可达）仿真表明，相对基线扫掠策略，所提方法在覆盖效率与可行性上显著更优，并为多艇协同覆盖奠定基础。

Autonomous sailboats excel at long-duration ocean observation, yet their performance is strongly anisotropic with respect to wind, and wind–current fields are spatially inhomogeneous and temporally varying—so that some regions may be only directionally accessible at certain times. Classical coverage path planning (CPP), built on static decomposition and boustrophedon sweeping, struggles under these constraints, while most sailboat planners address point-to-point routing rather than coverage.

We propose a dedicated spatiotemporal CPP framework. Spatially, topology-based morphological constraints keep the covered region compact and continuous, reducing fragmentation and backtracking. Temporally, forecast-aware look-ahead planning anticipates environmental evolution under accumulating forecast uncertainty. The ocean is gridded; a 16-direction action set and VPP-based effective velocity (including currents) define feasible moves. Simulations in stochastic inhomogeneous, time-varying seas—including partial directional accessibility—show clear gains in efficiency and feasibility over boustrophedon baselines, and establish a foundation for multi-sailboat cooperation.
