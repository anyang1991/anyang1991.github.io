翼帆无人单体帆船常按任务载荷与目标海域定制，总体构型（帆、船体、龙骨尺寸匹配）需在安全与效率间折中。设计空间大且难以从历史方案获得可靠性能先验，使传统离线代理仿真驱动设计需要过量 CFD 采样，计算代价过高。

本文提出融合贝叶斯优化与知识迁移的通用仿真驱动框架：BO 将采样自适应聚焦关键工况附近，KT 代理复用历史 CFD 信息，从而在无初始设计与先验估计时仍可高质量、低成本优化总体构型。在“Seagull”原型约束下（如横倾角 ≤26°），优化方案迎风航速提升约 56%、顺风约 106%；相较离线方法，仿真成本可降低约 40–60%。该框架为任务定制无人帆船提供可推广的总体设计方法，并有望延伸至水翼耦合等更复杂系统。

Wing-sailed autonomous monohulls are often mission-specific; general configuration—size matching among sail, hull, and keel—must trade safety against efficiency. Large design spaces and scarce prior performance estimates make classical offline surrogate-based simulation-driven design prohibitively expensive in CFD samples.

This paper introduces a generalised framework that couples Bayesian optimisation (BO) with knowledge transfer (KT): BO focuses sampling near critical operating points, while KT surrogates reuse historical CFD data, enabling high-quality, low-cost optimisation without an initial design or prior estimates. Applied to the “Seagull” prototype under design constraints (e.g., heel ≤26°), the optimised layout improves upwind speed by about 56% and downwind by about 106%, cutting simulation cost by roughly 40–60% versus offline methods. The approach offers a general design methodology for mission-specific autonomous sailboats, with potential extension to hydrofoil-coupled systems.
