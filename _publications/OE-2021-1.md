无人帆船以风能为推进来源，具有续航能力强、水声干扰小等优势，近二十年来已广泛应用于海底测绘、生物调查与长期海气观测等任务。既有综述多侧重于平台形态的罗列，较少将设计选择与海洋科学应用场景下的性能需求进行系统关联。

本文围绕结构耐久性、能源自持能力、抗倾覆能力与航速四项关键性能，梳理现有设计逻辑及其内在权衡：例如以翼帆与冗余舵增强结构耐久性，以自调翼与太阳能补充改善能源平衡，以压载龙骨保障稳性，以及由此形成的航速与稳性之间的矛盾。统计分析进一步指出当前瓶颈，包括任务场景权重考虑不足、航速与抗倾覆能力之间的固有对立，以及系统化设计优化框架的缺失。在此基础上，本文展望场景定制设计、可缩帆与可变帆及稳性装置等自适应机构，以及仿真驱动的设计优化等方向，以期为生态、生物地球化学与气象等深远海研究提供更为有力的观测平台支撑。

Autonomous sailboats are wind-powered platforms characterised by favourable endurance and low acoustic disturbance. Over the past two decades they have been widely employed in seafloor mapping, biological surveys, and persistent ocean–atmosphere observation. Existing reviews have largely catalogued platform configurations, while comparatively few studies have systematically linked design choices to the performance requirements of marine science applications.

This paper reviews present designs with respect to four critical performances—structural durability, energy self-sufficiency, overturning resistance, and sailing speed—and clarifies the underlying design logic and trade-offs. Illustrative measures include wing sails and redundant rudders for durability, self-trimming wings and solar harvesting for energy balance, and ballast keels for stability, together with the resulting tension between speed and stability. Statistical analysis further identifies key limitations, including insufficient weighting of mission scenarios, the inherent conflict between sailing speed and capsize resistance, and the absence of systematic design-optimisation frameworks. On this basis, the review outlines prospective directions such as scenario-customised design, adaptive mechanisms (including reefable and variant sails and stabilising devices), and simulation-based design optimisation, with the aim of providing stronger platform support for research in ecosystems, biogeochemistry, and meteorology.
