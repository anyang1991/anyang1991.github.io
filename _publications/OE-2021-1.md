无人帆船以风能为推进来源，续航长、噪声低，近二十年已广泛用于海底测绘、生物调查与长期海气观测等任务。已有综述多罗列平台形态，较少将设计选择与海洋科学场景下的性能需求系统挂钩。

本文按结构耐久性、能源自持能力、抗倾覆能力与航速四项关键性能梳理现有设计逻辑：例如以翼帆与冗余舵提升耐久、以自调翼与太阳能缓解能耗、以压载龙骨保障稳性，以及由此带来的航速–稳性权衡。统计分析进一步指出瓶颈：任务场景权重考虑不足、航速与抗倾覆固有对立，以及缺乏系统优化框架。据此展望场景定制设计、可缩/可变帆与稳性装置等自适应机构，以及仿真驱动的设计优化，以期支撑生态、生物地球化学与气象等深远海研究。

Autonomous sailboats harness wind for long endurance and low acoustic impact, and over two decades have supported mapping, biological surveys, and persistent ocean–atmosphere observation. Prior surveys list platforms but seldom link design choices to performance needs of marine-science missions.

This paper reviews designs through four critical lenses—structural durability, energy self-sufficiency, overturning resistance, and sailing speed—clarifying design logic (e.g., wing sails and redundant rudders for durability; self-trimming wings and solar harvest for energy; ballast keels for stability; and the resulting speed–stability trade-off). Statistical analysis highlights bottlenecks: insufficient scenario weighting, inherent speed–capsize conflict, and missing systematic optimisation frameworks. Future directions include scenario-customised designs, adaptive mechanisms (reefable/variant sails and stabilisers), and simulation-based optimisation to make sailboats stronger tools for ecosystems, biogeochemistry, and meteorology research.
