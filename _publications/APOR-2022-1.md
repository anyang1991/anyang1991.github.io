航速决定无人单体帆船的区域通过性、覆盖效率与目标跟踪能力，且与风向风速强耦合，无法用单一“平均航速”或某一分系统直观刻画。船体与龙骨多为任务导向的非常规设计，基于经验公式与梯度求解器的传统 VPP 易产生偏差，且常依赖性能先验。

本文提出面向无人单体帆船的通用 VPP：水动力建模可与 CFD 衔接；增强遗传算法（含末位优化）在无先验估计时仍能较快全局收敛；创新的 NIBO 策略利用相邻真风速/真风向状态的连续性，避免逐状态孤立求解，从而加速并平滑预测。原型应用及数值与海试结果表明，该工具尤其适合概念设计阶段对不同船体–帆装–龙骨组合做定量对比，为后续优化与任务规划提供评价基础。

Sailing speed governs trafficability, coverage efficiency, and tracking ability for autonomous sailing monohulls, yet it is strongly coupled to wind and cannot be reduced to a single average or to one subsystem. Task-oriented, unconventional hull–keel designs make classical VPPs based on empirical formulae and gradient solvers error-prone and often dependent on prior performance guesses.

This paper proposes a generalised VPP for autonomous sailing monohulls. CFD-compatible hydrodynamics, an enhanced genetic-algorithm solver with end-place optimisation, and a neighbourhood information-based optimisation (NIBO) strategy that exploits continuity across neighbouring true-wind states enable fast, refined predictions without a priori estimates. Prototype application plus numerical and experimental results show the tool is practical for early-stage quantitative comparison of unconventional hull–sail–keel configurations, supporting subsequent optimisation and mission planning.
