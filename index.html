About

中国科学院大学机械电子工程工学博士，曾于清华大学深圳国际研究生院从事博士后研究，现任中国科学院深海科学与工程研究所工程师。

研究方向主要包括无人帆船系统优化设计、路径规划，以及面向人类竞技帆船操作经验的航行策略迁移，致力于提升复杂海况下无人帆船的海洋观测效率。

I am an Engineer at the Institute of Deep-sea Science and Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences, focusing on the performance and efficiency of autonomous sailboats in complex marine environments.

My academic background includes a Ph.D. in Mechatronic Engineering, with specialization in vessel performance via multidisciplinary design optimization. As a postdoctoral researcher, I pivoted toward intelligent navigation—bridging human sailing expertise and robotic control.

I am always open to collaboration on autonomous and competitive sailing.
